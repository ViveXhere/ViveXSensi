How to use

1. Sign in with your Google account.

2. Check Access Granted and your account details in Settings.

3. Run Performance Lab tests normally and treat the results as estimates.

4. Test settings in Free Fire one at a time; results may vary by device.

Warning

Performance Lab provides browser-based estimates; it does not measure actual Free Fire FPS or physical touchscreen latency.

No tool automatically changes your phone's sensitivity, DPI, or game settings.

Avoid third-party apps, modified game files, and unsafe “boosters.” Never share your account details or OTP with anyone.
